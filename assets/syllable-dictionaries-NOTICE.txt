NOH syllable dictionaries: notice

NOH's « Couper en syllabes » (subtitle editor) proposes a split from a
dictionary per language, then from the language's vowel rule. Two of these
dictionaries are built from public datasets when a release is built; the
datasets themselves are not part of NOH's source repository.

languages/en/dictionary.txt
  Source: Moby Hyphenation List by Grady Ward (Moby Project), file
  mhyph.txt.
  Licence: public domain. Placed in the public domain by grant from the
  author (January 2001).
  Changes made by NOH: the Mac Roman text is decoded; entries containing a
  space are dropped; words are lower-cased; the syllable mark (•) becomes a
  hyphen and a hyphen of the word itself is written doubled; entries with a
  part that has no vowel are dropped; written as "word<TAB>split" lines,
  sorted.

languages/fr/dictionary.txt
  Source: Lexique 3.83, http://www.lexique.org, by Boris New, Christophe
  Pallier and contributors (Lexique383.zip, member Lexique383.tsv, column
  orthosyll).
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode
  Please cite: New, B., Pallier, C., Brysbaert, M., Ferrand, L. (2004).
  Lexique 2: A New French Lexical Database. Behavior Research Methods,
  Instruments, & Computers, 36(3), 516-524.
  Changes made by NOH: only the orthosyll column is kept, as
  "word<TAB>split" lines, lower case, sorted; entries whose word contains a
  space or a hyphen, or whose split does not spell the word, are dropped;
  a part without a vowel is joined to the part before it (for example
  "in-vo-lu-tif-s" becomes "in-vo-lu-tifs"); entries still holding a part
  without a vowel are dropped.
  The built file languages/fr/dictionary.txt is an adaptation of Lexique
  3.83 and is shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0. No additional
  restrictions apply to it.

The sources, their SHA-256 and the expected built files are pinned in
assets/syllable-sources.lock.json; tools/build_syllable_dicts.py performs the
changes listed above.
